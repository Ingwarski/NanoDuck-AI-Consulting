## Prompt to apply in each sister repository

Inspect that repository's own `AGENTS.md`, Git root, remote, SDD sources, runtime, encrypted storage and hosting topology first. Reconcile its own product idea → PRD → context/terms → affected design/architecture → DoD → QA → development plan with the installed SDD pipeline and checker. Preserve its approved visual baseline, authentication, origin/CSRF and provider/billing boundaries. Port the behavior below to the target's corresponding modules; do not copy this checkout's local SQLite, TLS or process assumptions into a hosted runtime. Commit and push only the selected target repository after its tests and deployment rules are satisfied.

1. **Version new consultations.** At owner-message acceptance, copy exact settings/instruction revisions and set a version such as `parallel-v1` in the run snapshot (`src/server/index.mjs`). Keep existing accepted runs on their saved sequential contract until terminal; do not reinterpret their transcript or replace an in-progress run. An unknown contract version fails safely without deleting state. Preserve exact model, effort, subscription routes and user-selected specialist count/depth.
2. **Make Head the orchestrator.** Replace separate roster and per-role task calls with one Head plan (`src/server/consultation-parallel.mjs`, `src/server/parallel-contract.mjs`). Head sees every owner message/correction in order and emits distinct role, private guidance, concise task and earlier-task dependencies. The known role names are examples, not an allowlist. Validate count, unique role names and acyclic dependency references structurally. If metadata is malformed, request one structural repair retaining Head's own task prose; never invent a generic assignment. Commit each valid task as its own verbatim Head-to-consultant message before any slow public research. Do not show private role guidance in transcript/export/API progress.
3. **Build per-call context from the chat.** Treat saved owner messages as the one canonical request, including exact wording, order, corrections and attachment-capability notices. Give every Head, consultant and Critic invocation that owner context exactly once, plus the recipient's own task/role and only relevant dependency results, research or Critic orders. Do not create or send a duplicate request document or enable Claude file tools. Keep provider output policy and relevant accepted guidance; do not resend the whole business transcript as both `owner` and `discussion`. A provider with insufficient capacity fails truthfully without truncation.
4. **Run independent consultants together.** Schedule all assignments whose declared dependencies are committed with concurrent provider calls; do not use provider-native subagents. Every worker has its own provider invocation and the run's shared cancellation signal. Persist each completed answer on arrival, even when a sibling fails. Use a unique assignment/result ID, run generation and compare-and-swap work revision in one encrypted transaction that commits the work ledger and visible message together (`src/server/store.mjs`, `src/server/local-store.mjs`, `src/server/local-state.mjs`). Validate recipient/author links, current result and legal transitions. Stop fences late results; Retry starts only missing work. A process restart stops the old generation and requires explicit Continue.
5. **Use Critic once per team round.** After ready consultants finish, Critic reviews all current answers against the owner request and Head tasks. It returns a short team summary and zero or more material, addressed findings. Do not force a finding, question or reply for sound work. Each finding becomes a direct Critic-to-consultant order linked to its assignment and defective result revision, with exact issue and required correction. Only affected consultants answer, concurrently where independent. Preserve the order and response in the encrypted ledger and ordinary messages.
6. **Track fulfillment explicitly.** A consultant response never closes its own order. Critic evaluates the linked defective answer and correction/grounded objection once, recording `resolved_corrected`, `resolved_objection_upheld`, `open` or `blocked_evidence` with a specific reason and Critic message reference. Reject a claimed correction if it merely repeats the defective answer; request one Critic structural/assessment repair rather than silently accepting it. Keep unresolved orders open across later rounds; do not issue the same unresolved order repeatedly or create a hidden rework loop. Head's final synthesis names open/evidence-blocked issues and remains provisional. Test wrong recipient, stale generation, stale result, Stop, restart and retry.
7. **Honor owner-selected depth.** Fixed 1, 3 or 5 means that many substantive team reviews. Auto lets Head choose CLOSE/CONTINUE after each round, bounded at ten. A focused correction assessment is not another full team round. Remove mandatory specialist final speeches and redundant per-consultant Critic challenges. Head synthesizes the latest confirmed answers and actual Critic assessments, covering every owner deliverable and source/uncertainty. Do not claim consensus from fluent agreement or an unanswered order.
8. **Keep public research isolated.** Head's plan can request one minimal public query. Validate the outgoing query separately from private owner context; then send only the safe query into a web-enabled Codex step. Search failure or unsafe query becomes an explicit evidence limitation, not a fabricated result or a reason to hide already committed Head assignments. Carry valid direct source metadata into the relevant answer/final message. The hosted target must preserve its own search/auth boundary.
9. **Update current guidance and UI only where necessary.** In `instructions/AGENTS.md`, `instructions/CONSILIUM.md` and `instructions/RUNTIME_PROMPTS.md`, remove sequential/forced-exchange claims for new runs. Migrate only byte-exact shipped defaults in encrypted saved documents (`src/server/instruction-documents.mjs`, `src/server/instruction-bootstrap.mjs`, `src/server/prompt-contracts.mjs`); preserve owner edits, revisions and accepted snapshots. In `public/index.html`, describe fixed rounds and Auto accurately. In `src/client/app.js`, safely display Head-created role names/initials and progress from a redacted public run summary. Keep the approved P5 Stop, notification sound, mobile/keyboard behavior and export layout.
10. **Verify before activation.** Extend coordinator, contract, local-store, HTTP and browser tests (`test/parallel-consultation.test.mjs`, `test/local-store.test.mjs`, `test/http.test.mjs`, `scripts/browser-check.mjs`) for dynamic roles, overlapping workers, faster-first visibility, user-context once, partial failure, legal Critic resolution and repeated-answer rejection, fixed/Auto depth, Stop, restart and no duplicate messages. Measure provider call count and prompt/context bytes on equivalent synthetic cases; report actual token categories only when the provider exposes them. Use Chromium and WebKit (plus Firefox/Edge where available), and distinguish local fixtures from live provider/physical-device evidence. Inspect final diff, SDD checker, active-run state and deployment safety before restart. Never interrupt an owner's live consultation merely to activate the change.

## Codex progress deadline correction — 24 September 2026

Port this correction independently of the Head-first research ordering fix. The local runtime logged `provider_timeout` for public research and a specialist position, then displayed the generic selected-route failure. The adapter imposed a fixed 540,000 ms wall-clock cutoff and collapsed its timeout into `provider_unavailable`. Historical logs did not record progress, so they cannot prove whether those particular calls were still advancing. Do not diagnose authentication, model entitlement or quota from this generic message alone.

1. In `src/server/codex-provider.mjs`, replace the single completion wall-clock timer with an inactivity watchdog and an independent absolute ceiling. Reference defaults: 540,000 ms without progress; 1,800,000 ms total. Put this timer lifecycle in `src/server/turn-deadline.mjs`. Allow short budgets through an internal constructor argument for tests, not through owner prompts. Preserve the exact accepted model/effort and the existing subscription route. Do not apply this change blindly to Claude or change its existing deadline.
2. Start the watchdog when `turn/start` returns its turn ID. Renew only on the expected thread AND turn: nonempty `item/agentMessage/delta`, `item/reasoning/textDelta`, `item/reasoning/summaryTextDelta`, or `item/started` / `item/completed` carrying an item ID. Unrelated turns, empty deltas, account messages and generic heartbeats cannot extend it. Deltas indicate activity only; a matching successful terminal event remains necessary before committing an answer. Never log or persist hidden reasoning or tool payloads.
3. Race terminal completion against inactivity expiry, absolute expiry, connection closure and cancellation. Clear both watchdog timers on every exit. Preserve immediate Stop, process-tree termination, temporary credential cleanup and generation fencing. Continuing progress cannot extend the absolute ceiling. Do not automatically resubmit timed-out work or silently reduce reasoning effort.
4. Preserve `provider_idle_timeout` and `provider_timeout` as separate result codes rather than converting them to `provider_unavailable`. In `src/server/consultation.mjs`, explain respectively that the call stopped reporting progress or reached its maximum waiting time; state that the question/confirmed replies remain saved and name the actual Retry control. Log only safe error category, operation, duration, idle duration and progress count. Existing historic System messages remain unchanged.
5. Extend `test/fixtures/fake-codex.mjs` and `test/codex-provider.test.mjs`: a progressing call finishes after its idle duration; only wrong-turn activity expires; endless valid progress still hits the absolute ceiling. Add timer-disposal and progress-filter tests in `test/turn-deadline.test.mjs`. Retain cancellation, early completion, connection loss, failed-turn and credential-cleanup regressions. In `test/consultation.test.mjs`, fail a specialist with each timeout code, check the precise System message, Retry to completion, preserve the complete prefix, avoid repeated Head assignments and keep the exact model/effort.
6. Reconcile the target's model-settings/product/PRD/architecture/QA/development-plan records through their owners. Replace old claims of a universal fixed nine-minute cutoff, retain historical incident evidence, refresh SDD provenance and preserve the approved visual baseline. Run relevant automated and real-browser recovery checks. A short successful live call proves current connectivity, not completion of the owner's long consultation or a real thirty-minute duration test.
7. Activate only in the selected target repository/runtime. Before restarting, inspect active-run state; do not interrupt active work. Preserve the encrypted store and provider grants. After restart verify HTTPS health and offer Retry on the failed conversation; do not send the owner's saved task again without an explicit resume action.

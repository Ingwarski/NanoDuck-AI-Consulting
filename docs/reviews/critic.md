# Critic review and resolution

13 September 2026. A separately launched Critic reviewed the synthesized product brief and deployment boundary against the two source reviewers' findings. Scope: hidden complexity, retained requirements, truthful real-agent/research/model claims, public-repository privacy and the login-and-work promise. No second broad code audit was performed.

Two corrections were requested and applied:

1. Acceptance must not demand a disagreement on every sound recommendation. The brief now tests a scenario with a material weakness that deserves critique and a separate scenario that permits well-supported agreement.
2. History retention must be explicit. The brief now retains conversations and attachments encrypted without a time limit, until the owner deletes them.

The Critic read those two edits and confirmed that both conditions were satisfied. Its conclusion is acceptance **for owner review within the design-only scope**, not owner approval, implementation authorization or proof that the backend exists.

The actual exchange is preserved verbatim in the local ignored review-session transcript. This public document is the concise resolution record.
